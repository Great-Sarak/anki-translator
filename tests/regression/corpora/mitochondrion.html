Function

The mitochondrion generates most of the cell's supply of ATP, which is used throughout the cell as a source of chemical energy.

Mitochondria contain their own genome, mtDNA, which in humans encodes 37 genes including 13 proteins of the oxidative phosphorylation chain, 22 transfer RNAs, and 2 ribosomal RNAs — several distinct facts that cannot fit one card.
